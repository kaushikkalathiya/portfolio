Culinary Platform Case Study

Gerum Daginn Girnilegan: Recipe & Culinary Platform

The Challenge

The client wanted an intuitive recipe platform in Iceland to share detailed cooking recipes, ingredient measurements, and step-by-step instructions for home cooks.

Implemented Engineering Solution

  • Recipe Content Engine: Built custom WordPress content fields to easily input ingredients, preparation times, and cooking steps.
  • Recipe of the Week & PDF Downloads: Integrated automatic recipe highlighting and custom PDF generation for home printing.
  • Intuitive UI/UX: Enhanced mobile usability so users can easily follow recipes while cooking in the kitchen.
